Death of Ellen Elizabeth (Winter) Darcy, John F. Darcy’s first wife

Rhode Island U.S., Death Registrations and Records, 1852–1946: Deaths Registered in the City of Providence, 1885 (Ancestry collection 62906, record 259226, image i3812998-00209); Rhode Island, U.S., Death Index, 1630–1930 (collection 4264, record 68634)

What this document is

A death record kept by the state of Rhode Island.

What it says

City of Providence death register, 1885. December 7: Ellen Elizabeth Darcy, 23 years 9 months, residence 78 Borden Street, female, married, wife of John, born in Ireland; parents Matthew and Mary Winters; cause: consumption. Ancestry index: Ellen Elizabeth Darey [Ellen Elizabeth Winters], 23, born abt 1862 Ireland, died 7 Dec 1885, Providence. A separate Rhode Island death index card reads: Ellen E. Darcy, wf (wife), kin John Darcy, Rose Darcy, died 07 Dec 1885, 23 yrs.

Place

78 Borden Street, Providence, Rhode Island
