1880 U.S. census: James Stewart’s household in Fall River, with daughters Sarah and Margaret (probably the Nodders girls)

1880 United States Federal Census

Open the original record

No picture of this record is kept on this site yet.

What this document is

The United States census. A census taker went door to door and wrote down every person in each household, with age, birthplace and work.

What it says

James Stewart, 36, head, born Scotland; Mary Stewart, 38, wife, born England; Sarah, 17; Margrette, 10, daughter, born Massachusetts, at school; Connie, 7; Charles, 5. This is a candidate only. It matches Margaret Jane’s age, her Massachusetts birth and her mother Mary’s English birth, and it would explain why her daughter Ethel’s death certificate gives her maiden name as Stewart. Nothing yet proves this is her family. UPDATE: now very likely the right family. Margaret’s mother Mary Regan married James H. Stewart, born Scotland, in December 1869; Margaret’s sister Sarah Ann Nodders married in Fall River in 1882; and the ages (James 36 born Scotland, Mary 38 born England, Margaret 10 born Massachusetts) fit. Age conflicts remain: James should be about 32–33 and Margaret 11.

Place

87 Tecumseh Street, Fall River, Massachusetts (dwelling 187)
