1930 U.S. census: Eugenie Darcy and her children, 111 Pratt Street, Providence

1930 United States Federal Census

Open the original record

The original record, as it appears in the register or index.

What this document is

The United States census. A census taker went door to door and wrote down every person in each household, with age, birthplace and work.

What it says

Enumerated 26 April 1930 by John W. Crawford. Eugenie Darcy (indexed "Eugene Darcey"), head, rents for $28 a month, no radio, F, W, 35, divorced, married first at 17, reads and writes, born Canada (French), parents born Canada (French), mother tongue French, came 1898, naturalized, speaks English; interior decorator, rug maker(s) industry, wage worker, at work. Constance, daughter, F, W, 17, single, attending school, reads and writes, born Rhode Island, father born Rhode Island, mother born Canada (French), occupation none. Albert N., son, M, W, 14, single, at school, born Massachusetts.

Place

111 Pratt Street, Ward 1, Providence, Rhode Island (block 27, tract 43, ED 4-126, sheet 7B(?), dwelling 25, family 66)

How it was read

Read from the census image. Eugenie’s arrival year here (1898) differs from her mother’s declaration (October 1899).
