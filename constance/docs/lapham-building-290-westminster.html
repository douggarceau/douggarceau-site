290 Westminster Street was the Lapham Building, home to several beauty shops

The Providence Directory, 1937 and 1938 (Internet Archive items providencedirect1937unse, providencedirect1938unse, full text)

The original record, as it appears in the register or index.

What this document is

A city directory: a yearly book, like a phone book, listing the adults of the city with their work and home address.

What it says

Providence directory (1937–38 editions), buildings list: “Lapham Building 290 Westminster.” The same directory lists, at that address: Anton’s (Anton Kienast), beauty shop, 290 Westminster, room 402; the Lapham Beauty Shoppe, room 605; and the Lapham Real Estate Co. Inc., room 510. Constance worked as a hairdresser in this building, in room 506 in 1937 and in room 402 (printed “290 Wash”) in 1938. Pictures: an early-1900s colored postcard captioned “Corner of Westminster and Mathewson Sts. showing Lapham Building, Providence, R.I.,” with LAPHAM BUILDING lettered over the entrance; and an undivided-back era postcard captioned “Westminster Street — Providence, R.I.,” showing streetcars and crowds on Westminster Street. Both postcards date from decades before Constance worked there and are shown for the building and street, not for her.

Place

Lapham Building, 290 Westminster Street, Providence
