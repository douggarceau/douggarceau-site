What this document is

A marriage record, written when the couple married.

What it says

City of Providence marriage register for 1884. April 17: John Francis Darcy, residence Providence, 23, jeweler, born in Providence, son of John and Catherine, first marriage; Ellen Elizabeth Winter, residence Providence, 21, born in Ireland, daughter of Matthew and Mary, first marriage. Same name, city, trade (jeweler) and parents (John and Catherine) as the groom who married Margaret Nodders in 1888, and the ages agree (23 in 1884, 26 in January 1888). Ellen died in December 1885 (see her death record). The 1888 register still marks John’s marriage to Margaret as a first marriage.

Place

Providence, Rhode Island
