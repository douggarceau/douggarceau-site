Célina (Dufresne) Delaire: birth, census years and death

1901, 1911, 1921 Census of Canada; Rhode Island Death Registrations; Vermont Death Records

Open the original record

No picture of this record is kept on this site yet.

What this document is

The census of Canada. A census taker went door to door and wrote down every person in each household.

What it says

1901 Canada census: Celina G. Delaire, wife, 53, born 27 May 1847, Québec, French, Roman Catholic, reads, writes and speaks English. 1911 and 1921 Canada censuses: Selma / Celina Delaire, wife, living at Brome (born May 1847, Quebec). A Rhode Island death record: Selena Dufresne, wife of Edward Delaire, mother of Selena Upton, Providence, November 1942. A Vermont death record also exists for a Seline Delaine, maiden name Dufresne, born Brome, Quebec, wife of Edward, mother of Peter John Delane. (The 1891 census gave her birthplace as the United States; the later censuses say Quebec.)

Place

Brome, Quebec; Providence, R.I.

How it was read

Index only; the Rhode Island and Vermont death records may describe two different women and need the images to settle which is Julia’s mother.
