1940 United States Federal Census

Open the original record

No picture of this record is kept on this site yet.

What this document is

The United States census. A census taker went door to door and wrote down every person in each household, with age, birthplace and work.

What it says

Norman A. D’Arcy, 24, head, married, born Massachusetts, rents for $15 a month, completed 4 years of high school; attendant at a parking lot, 65 hours the week before, 52 weeks worked in 1939, wages $988; in 1935 lived in Providence. Claire M., wife, 23. A daughter, 1.

Place

110 Beach Avenue, Warwick, Rhode Island (sheet 12A, household 332)

How it was read

Index; image not yet read. The child’s name is left off here: she may be living.
