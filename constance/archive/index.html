Scope and content

The collection documents the birth of Constance Eugenie Darcy at 410 Smith Street, Providence, on 12 October 1912; her mother’s family, the Deslières (Delaire) of Sutton and Bolton, Quebec; her father’s family, the Darcys of Providence and East Providence; her grandmother Margaret Jane Nodders of Lowell and her English parents, married at Oldham in 1862, and Margaret’s stepfather James H. Stewart; her work as a hairdresser on Westminster Street and later as a nurse; her marriage to Laurence Brown Douglas in Birmingham, Alabama, in 1939; and her life in San Francisco, Pittsburgh, Pawtucket, Providence, Warwick and East Greenwich until her death in 1994.

Arrangement

Documents are arranged in series by record type, and within each series by date. Each document has a permanent catalog number in date order.

  • Series 1. Civil and church registration (births, baptisms, marriages, deaths) (28 documents)
  • Series 2. Census and population registers (23 documents)
  • Series 3. Immigration, travel and border crossing (2 documents)
  • Series 4. Military and naturalization (5 documents)
  • Series 5. Directories (43 documents)
  • Series 6. Cemetery and burial records (11 documents)
  • Series 7. Newspapers and obituaries (3 documents)
  • Series 8. Indexes, banks and other records (1 documents)

Finding the records

Use the Catalog to see every document by series, the People index to find every document that names a person, and the Places index to find every document recorded in a place. Each document page gives the citation, what the record says, and the image where one is held.
