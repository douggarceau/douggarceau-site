What the record says

Under Douglas: "Constance E Mrs nurse Roger Williams Genl Hosp r644 River av"; Laurence B. Douglas (Constance E.), window displays, 644 River Avenue. Under Erickson in the same directory: "Eugenia Mrs r644 River av" and, on the next line, "Eugenie Mrs h107 Rugby". The first places Constance’s mother, Mrs. Erickson, rooming at Constance’s house.
