What the record says

Find a Grave memorials: Lillian Angelus Darcy Kinsley, died 9 Dec 1959, buried Saint Andrew’s Catholic Church Cemetery, Newtown, Bucks County, Pennsylvania (the memorial’s birth date, 6 Jul 1887, disagrees with her East Providence birth registration, 6 Aug 1892). Loretta Louise Darcy Harrop, born 19 Aug 1890, died Sep 1985, Oak Grove Cemetery, Pawtucket; spouses William Joseph Hogan (1886–1917) and James Edmund Harrop (1893–1945, married 1925). Harold Francis Darcy Sr., born 22 Apr 1898, died 6 Nov 1962 at Warwick, Mount Saint Mary, section L, lot 112, grave 10. Olive R. Darcy Reeves, 1901–1965. These are volunteer memorials; dates match the birth registers except where noted.
