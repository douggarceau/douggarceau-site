What the record says

City of Providence death register, 1885. October 2: Joseph Edward Darcy, 6 months, residence 5 Bogman Street, male, single, born in Providence; parents John F. and Ellen; cause: teething, convulsions. Index: Joseph Edward Darcy, age 6/12, born abt 1885 Providence, died 2 Oct 1885, father John F. Darcy, mother Ellen Darcy. He was a half-brother Albert Darcy never knew.
