What the record says

Albert Norman Darcy, 24, white, born December 7, 1915, Taunton, Massachusetts; lives in Warwick; employer New Jersey Parking Co.; next of kin his wife, Claire Marie Darcy. Height 5 ft 11 in, weight 155, ruddy complexion, black hair, brown eyes. Registered October 16, 1940, the same day Laurence registered in San Francisco.
