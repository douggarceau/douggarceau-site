What the record says

City of Providence marriage register for 1888. January 13: John Francis Darcy, residence Providence, 26, jeweler, born in Providence (Irish column marked I.), son of John and Catherine, first marriage; Margaret Nodders, residence Providence, 19, born in Lowell, Mass. (marked E.), daughter of Henry and Mary, first marriage. Married by Patrick F. McKenna, R.C. clergyman. NOTE: this register names Margaret’s father as Henry; her 1942 death certificate (informant her son Albert) names him William Nodder. Ancestry’s index transcribes the same entry: John Francis Darcy, 26, born abt 1862 Providence, father John Darcy, mother Catherine Darcy, spouse Margaret Nodders, 13 Jan 1888.
