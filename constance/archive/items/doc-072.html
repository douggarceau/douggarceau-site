What the record says

Jefferson County marriage license record book 154, p. 409. Lawrence Douglas, salesman, of 1860 South Hamlin Avenue, Chicago; Constance Darcy of 454 Broadway, Providence. Her parents Albert B. Darcy and Eugenie Delaire. Married October 9, 1939 by Thomas R. Lea, municipal court judge. Witness lines blank.
