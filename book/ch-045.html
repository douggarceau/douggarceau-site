A Rudiment Routine

You don’t need all forty rudiments every day. This routine covers the main families in about ten minutes. Use the metronome and start slow.

TIMEWHAT TO PLAY
2 minSingle strokes (Chapter 27), both hands leading.
2 minDouble strokes (Chapter 28).
2 minParadiddles (Chapter 29).
2 minFlam taps or flam accents (Chapters 33–34).
2 minFive- and nine-stroke rolls (Chapters 38 and 40).
EXAMPLE 45.1Warm-up combination
TEMPO

Singles, doubles and paradiddles in one exercise.

RotateTIP

Pick two or three different rudiments each week to add to the routine. By the end of a few months you will have played them all.
