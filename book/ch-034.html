The Flam Accent

The flam accent is a flam followed by two taps, in triplets: lR L R, rL R L. The flam alternates hands each beat.

Bass drum on every beat under the flams.

Think single strokesTIP

Under the flams, the flam accent is just a single stroke roll in triplets: R L R L R L. The grace notes sit on top.
