The Four Basic Strokes

Every note you play is one of four strokes. Knowing them lets you play loud and soft notes next to each other without effort.

STROKESTARTSENDSUSE
FullHighHighLoud notes in a row
DownHighLowA loud note followed by soft ones
TapLowLowSoft notes
UpLowHighA soft note that prepares a loud one

The trick is where the stick stops. After a down stroke, catch the stick low, just above the head. Before an accent, lift the stick during the soft note that comes first (the up stroke).

EXAMPLE 5.1Full strokes
TEMPO

All notes loud, sticks starting and ending high.

EXAMPLE 5.2Down, tap, tap, up
TEMPO

An accent at the start of each beat. Down stroke on the accent, taps in the middle, up stroke on the last note of the group.

Two heights onlyTIP

Use just two heights: high for accents (about 12 inches), low for taps (about 2 inches). Clear heights make clear dynamics.
