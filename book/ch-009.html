How to Practice

Twenty minutes every day beats two hours once a week. Your hands and brain learn during the days in between, so regular practice adds up fast.

TIMEWHAT TO PLAY
5 minWarm up: single and double strokes, slowly.
10 minToday’s chapter: the examples, slow then faster.
5 minReview one older chapter you like.
5 minJust play: grooves, fills, or along with a song.
EXAMPLE 9.1Warm-up
TEMPO

Eighth notes, alternating hands. Start at 60 and raise the tempo 5 at a time.

  • Practice slowly enough to play it right every time.
  • Stop when you make the same mistake twice. Slow down and fix that one spot.
  • Record yourself on a phone now and then. You will hear things you miss while playing.
  • End each session with something fun.

Protect your earsTIP

Real drums are loud enough to damage hearing over time. Wear earplugs or isolating headphones when you practice on a kit.
