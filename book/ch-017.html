Sixteenth Notes

Sixteenth notes split each beat into four. They have two beams (or two flags). Count them “1 e & a, 2 e & a”.

EXAMPLE 17.1Straight sixteenths
TEMPO

Alternate hands, very even.

EXAMPLE 17.2With the bass drum
TEMPO

Sixteenths in the hands, quarter notes on the bass drum.

EXAMPLE 17.3Eighths to sixteenths
TEMPO

Two beats of eighths, two beats of sixteenths. The hands double their speed but the beat stays the same.

Small and relaxedTIP

Faster notes need smaller strokes, not more effort. Let the sticks bounce.
