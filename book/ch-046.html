The Basic Rock Beat

The basic rock beat is the groove behind thousands of songs. Your right hand plays eighth notes on the hi-hat, your right foot plays the bass drum on 1 and 3, and your left hand plays the snare on 2 and 4.

EXAMPLE 46.1Hi-hat alone
TEMPO

Right hand, eighth notes. Count 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &.

EXAMPLE 46.2Add the bass drum
TEMPO

Bass drum on 1 and 3, played together with the hi-hat.

EXAMPLE 46.3Add the snare
TEMPO

The complete beat: snare on 2 and 4.

Build it in layersTIP

If the full beat falls apart, go back to the hi-hat alone, then add one limb at a time. This is how every new groove should be learned.
