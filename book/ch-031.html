The Paradiddle-Diddle

The paradiddle-diddle is two singles followed by two doubles: R L R R L L. Unlike the paradiddle, it always starts with the same hand.

Singles on the snare, first double on the rack tom, second double on the floor tom.

A drummer’s favoriteTIP

Played fast around the kit, the paradiddle-diddle is behind many famous fills. Once it’s smooth, try moving the doubles to different drums.
