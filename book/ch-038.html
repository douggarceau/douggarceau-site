The Five-Stroke Roll

A five-stroke roll is two double strokes followed by an accented single: R R L L R. It is written as a note with slashes (the roll) tied to an accented note.

EXAMPLE 38.1Five-stroke rolls
TEMPO

Each roll starts on the beat and lands on the &. Then start again with the other hand: LLRR L.

EXAMPLE 38.2Written out
TEMPO

The same roll written as individual notes, slowly. The doubles are 32nd notes; here they are shown as sixteenths at half speed.

Ends on the accentTIP

Every short roll ends with an accented single stroke. Aim for that last note: the doubles just lead into it.
