Where to Go Next

If you’ve worked through this book, you can hold the sticks, read rhythms, play the main rudiments, groove in many styles, play fills and read a chart. That’s a real foundation. Here’s how to keep growing.

  • Play along with records. Pick songs you love and learn the drum parts by ear.
  • Play with other people. Nothing teaches time and listening like a band.
  • Go deeper into a style. Jazz, funk, Latin and metal each have whole books of their own. There are excellent method books for every style.
  • Keep your routine. Rudiments and reading, a little every day.
  • Find a teacher. A good teacher spots in minutes what you might take months to notice.
