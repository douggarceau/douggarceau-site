Holding the Sticks

This book uses matched grip: both hands hold the sticks the same way. It is what most drummers use on the drum set today.

  1. Find the balance point about a third of the way up from the butt end of the stick.
  2. Pinch the stick at that point between the pad of your thumb and the first joint of your index finger. This pinch is called the fulcrum.
  3. Let your other fingers wrap loosely around the stick. They guide it; they do not squeeze it.
  4. Turn your hands so the backs face mostly up (German grip) or halfway to the side (American grip). Both are fine.
  5. Leave a small gap between the stick and your palm so it can move freely.

A good grip lets the stick bounce off the drum on its own. If you squeeze, the stick chokes and your hands get tired fast.

EXAMPLE 3.1Alternating strokes
TEMPO

Right, left, right, left in steady eighth notes. Keep both sticks at the same height and the sound of both hands the same.

Check yourselfTIP

Look at your hands in a mirror while you play. Both sticks should make the same shape in the air. If one hand looks different, slow down and match it to the other.
