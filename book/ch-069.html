Part 5 · Other Styles · Chapter 69

Samba

Samba is fast, joyful and felt in two. The bass drum imitates the big surdo drum with a pattern on 1, the a of 1, 2 and the a of 2, while the hands play steady sixteenths.

EXAMPLE 69.1Samba feet
TEMPO

The bass drum surdo pattern with the hi-hat foot on the &s.

EXAMPLE 69.2Samba groove
TEMPO

Add sixteenths on the snare with accents.

Feet firstTIP

Get the bass drum and hi-hat foot pattern going on their own until it feels like walking. Then add the hands.
