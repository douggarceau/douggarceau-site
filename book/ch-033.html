Flam Taps

A flam tap is a flam followed by a tap with the same hand as the main note: lR R, rL L. It sounds like a galloping double stroke.

EXAMPLE 33.1Flam taps
TEMPO
EXAMPLE 33.2Flam taps with bass drum
TEMPO

Bass drum on every beat under the flam taps.

Two heights, againTIP

The main note is high, the tap is low, and the low tap stick then plays the grace note of the next flam. Keep those two heights clear and flam taps become easy.
