Eighth-Note Triplets

A triplet fits three notes into one beat instead of two. It is marked with a 3 over the beam. Count “1 trip-let, 2 trip-let”.

EXAMPLE 23.1Triplets
TEMPO

Alternate hands. Notice that the lead hand switches every beat.

EXAMPLE 23.2Accent the beat
TEMPO

Accent the first note of each triplet.

EXAMPLE 23.3Triplets over the bass drum
TEMPO

Bass drum on every beat. Feel three even notes inside each beat.

Say itTIP

“Straw-ber-ry, straw-ber-ry” has the same rhythm as triplets.
