The 6/8 Afro-Cuban Bell

Much Afro-Cuban and West African music is built on a 6/8 bell pattern: seven notes spread over twelve eighth notes (1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12). On the drum set it is often played on the ride cymbal or its bell.

EXAMPLE 70.1Bell pattern
TEMPO

The ride plays the bell pattern; the bass drum plays the four big beats softly.

EXAMPLE 70.2With hi-hat foot
TEMPO

Hi-hat foot on beats 2 and 4.

Sing the bellTIP

Sing the pattern until you can repeat it for a whole minute without thinking. Then add the feet.
