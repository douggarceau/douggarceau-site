Hi-Hat on 2 and 4

Add the hi-hat pedal on beats 2 and 4. It clicks together with the ride on those beats and gives the band a strong reference point.

EXAMPLE 78.1Ride and hi-hat foot
TEMPO

A crisp chickTIP

Press the pedal down firmly and keep it closed until the next note. The sound should be a short “chick”.
