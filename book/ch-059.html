Crashes and Song Sections

Drummers use the crash cymbal to mark the start of new sections: verse, chorus, bridge. The crash is almost always played together with the bass drum on beat 1.

EXAMPLE 59.1Crash on the 1
TEMPO

Crash on beat 1 of the first bar, then back to the hi-hat.

EXAMPLE 59.2Set-up and crash
TEMPO

A snare on the & of 4 sets up the crash in the next bar.

Know the formTIP

Listen to a song and count the bars of each section. Most sections are 4, 8 or 16 bars long. Crash where the new section starts.
