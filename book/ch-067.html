Reggae: The One Drop

The one drop is the classic reggae groove. The bass drum and snare land together on beat 3 only, and beat 1 is left empty. The snare is usually played as a cross-stick: the tip of the stick rests on the head and the shaft clicks on the rim.

EXAMPLE 67.1One drop
TEMPO

Snare (cross-stick) and bass drum together on 3.

EXAMPLE 67.2One drop with open hat
TEMPO

Resist beat 1TIP

Every instinct says to play the bass drum on 1. Don’t. The empty 1 is what makes the groove float.
