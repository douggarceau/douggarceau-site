Landing the Fill

The last note of a fill and the first note after it are what the band hears most. Make the landing strong and exactly on time.

  • Crash cymbal and bass drum together on beat 1.
  • Don’t leave a gap after the fill: the groove starts again right away.
  • Some fills end on the & of 4 with the crash on 1; others end with a note on 1 itself.

Practice the landingTIP

Practice just the last beat of the fill and the crash, over and over, until the landing is automatic.
