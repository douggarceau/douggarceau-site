The Shuffle

A shuffle is built on triplets with the middle note left out: long-short, long-short. It is the heartbeat of blues, early rock and roll, and a lot of rock.

EXAMPLE 61.1Shuffle on the hi-hat
TEMPO

Play the first and last note of each triplet.

EXAMPLE 61.2The shuffle groove
TEMPO

Add bass drum on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4.

EXAMPLE 61.3Shuffle with a busier foot
TEMPO

The bass drum plays the shuffle rhythm too on beat 3.

Feel, not mathTIP

A shuffle should bounce. Listen to blues records and sing along with the hi-hat before you play it.
