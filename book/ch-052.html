Sixteenth-Note Bass Drum

With eighth notes on the hi-hat, the bass drum can play on the e or a of the beat, between the hi-hat notes. These kicks give rock and funk grooves their bounce.

Count the gapsTIP

The hard part is kicks that fall between hi-hat notes. Count sixteenths out loud and move your hand in the air for the missing sixteenths if it helps.
