Eighth Rests

An eighth rest looks like a small 7 with a dot on its top-left. It is half a beat of silence. When an eighth rest is followed by an eighth note, the note falls on the &.

EXAMPLE 15.1Rest on the &
TEMPO

Play on the numbers only. (These look like quarter notes, because an eighth plus an eighth rest is one beat.)

EXAMPLE 15.2Play on the &
TEMPO

Rest on the numbers, play on the &s. Tap your foot on the numbers to keep your place.

EXAMPLE 15.3Mixed
TEMPO

A reading line with eighth rests in different places.

Say “rest”TIP

When counting Example 15.2, say the numbers quietly and the &s loudly: “(1) AND (2) AND”.
