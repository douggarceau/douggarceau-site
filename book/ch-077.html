The Jazz Ride Pattern

The ride pattern is the foundation of jazz drumming: quarter notes on 1 and 3, and “ding-a” on 2 and 4 (the skip note falls on the swung & of 2 and 4).

EXAMPLE 77.1Ride pattern
TEMPO

Sing it: “ding, ding-a-ding, ding-a”.

EXAMPLE 77.2Two bars
TEMPO

The ride leads the bandTIP

In jazz, the ride cymbal is the time. Bass players lock to it. Keep it steady and let everything else float on top.
