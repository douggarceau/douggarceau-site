Rests

A rest means don’t play, but keep counting. Every note value has a matching rest.

RESTLOOKS LIKELASTS
Quarter restA zigzag1 beat
Half restA block sitting on the middle line2 beats
Whole restA block hanging from the 4th lineA whole bar
EXAMPLE 13.1Quarter rests
TEMPO

Play on 1 and 3, rest on 2 and 4.

EXAMPLE 13.2Rests first
TEMPO

Rest on 1 and 2 (a half rest), then play 3 and 4.

EXAMPLE 13.3A bar of silence
TEMPO

Four notes, then a whole bar of rest. Keep counting through it.

Rests are activeTIP

Lift your stick slightly during a rest, ready for the next note. Freezing in place makes the next note late.
