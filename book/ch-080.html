Comping on the Snare

While the ride and hi-hat keep time, the left hand adds short snare notes called comping (from “accompanying”). Comping answers the soloist and pushes the band.

The method is simple: keep the time pattern completely steady and move a single comp note around it.

EXAMPLE 80.1Comp on the & of 2
TEMPO
EXAMPLE 80.2Comp on the & of 4
TEMPO
EXAMPLE 80.3Comp on the & of 1 and 3
TEMPO

These fall between ride notes: the hardest ones.

The ride doesn’t noticeTIP

If your ride pattern changes when the snare comes in, you’re not independent yet. Slow down until the ride stays exactly the same.
