Counting Out Loud

Counting out loud connects what you see on the page to what you play. It feels awkward at first. Do it anyway: every good reader started this way.

NOTENOTES PER BEATSAY
Quarter notes11 2 3 4
Eighth notes21 & 2 & 3 & 4 &
Sixteenth notes41 e & a, 2 e & a…
Triplets31 trip-let, 2 trip-let…
EXAMPLE 10.1Count quarters
TEMPO

Say “1 2 3 4” as you play.

EXAMPLE 10.2Count eighths
TEMPO

Say “1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &”.

EXAMPLE 10.3Count sixteenths
TEMPO

Say “1 e & a”. “e” rhymes with “me”, “a” sounds like “uh”.

Say the rests tooTIP

Keep counting through rests, even though you don’t play. The count is what keeps you in the right place.
