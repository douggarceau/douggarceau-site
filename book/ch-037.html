The Single Drag Tap

The single drag tap is a drag followed by a tap with the other hand: llR L, rrL R. The main note of the drag is accented.

EXAMPLE 37.1Single drag taps
TEMPO
EXAMPLE 37.2Two bars with bass drum
TEMPO

Accent and tapTIP

Big accent on the drag, small tap after it. The contrast is what makes it sound musical.
