Brushes

Brushes are fans of thin wires (or nylon) used instead of sticks for soft music. The left brush usually sweeps in circles across the snare to make a steady “shhh”, while the right brush taps the ride pattern on the snare.

  • Left hand: sweep a slow circle on the head, clockwise, one full circle every two beats.
  • Right hand: tap the swing ride pattern on the snare.
  • Hi-hat foot on 2 and 4, bass drum feathered.
