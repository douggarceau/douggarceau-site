Funk Basics

Funk is built on the sixteenth-note grid. The hi-hat usually stays simple while the bass drum plays syncopated sixteenths against a strong backbeat.

Tight and shortTIP

Funk notes are short and precise. Keep the hi-hat closed tight and play the bass drum with the beater bouncing off the head.
