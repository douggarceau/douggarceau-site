What Are Rudiments?

Rudiments are short sticking patterns that every drummer learns, the way piano players learn scales. They build control, speed and evenness, and nearly every fill and groove you will ever play is made from them.

There are forty standard rudiments. They fall into four families:

FAMILYBUILT FROMEXAMPLES
RollsSingle, double or buzzed strokesSingle stroke roll, double stroke roll, five-stroke roll
DiddlesA mix of singles and doublesParadiddle, double paradiddle, paradiddle-diddle
FlamsA soft grace note just before a main noteFlam, flam tap, flam accent, flamacue
DragsTwo soft grace notes before a main noteDrag, single drag tap

TEMPO

The most basic rudiment of all. Right, left, right, left.

Slow, then fast, then slowTIP

The classic way to practice a rudiment: start very slowly, speed up gradually to as fast as you can play it cleanly, then slow back down. This is called “open-close-open”.
