Feathering the Bass Drum

Jazz drummers often play the bass drum very softly on all four beats. This is called feathering. It should be felt more than heard, supporting the bass player without covering them.

EXAMPLE 79.1Ride, hi-hat and feathered bass drum
TEMPO

The bass drum notes are ghost notes: barely touch the head.

Heel downTIP

Feathering is easiest heel down, using just the ankle. Let the beater touch the head and come straight back off.
