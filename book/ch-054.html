Four on the Floor

In four on the floor, the bass drum plays every beat. With open hi-hats on the &s, it becomes the classic disco and dance groove.

A steady footTIP

Four on the floor exposes any unevenness in your bass drum. Practice with the metronome until every kick lines up with the click.
