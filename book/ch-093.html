Fill Workshop

Every fill in this part was built the same way: pick a rhythm (quarters, eighths, sixteenths or triplets), pick a length (one beat, two beats or a whole bar), and pick a path around the drums.

CHOOSEOPTIONS
RhythmQuarters, eighths, sixteenths, triplets, or a mix
Length1 beat, 2 beats, 1 bar
PathHigh to low, low to high, snare only, back and forth
ExtrasFlams, accents, bass drum notes, rests
EXAMPLE 93.1Flam fill
TEMPO

Flams on the toms mixed with sixteenths.

EXAMPLE 93.2Paradiddle fill
TEMPO

Paradiddle sticking with the right hand on the floor tom.

Write them downTIP

When you make up a fill you like, write it down. In a few weeks you’ll have your own fill book.
