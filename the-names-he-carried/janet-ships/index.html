Janet's Ships

Janet Crombie (born Janet Page Douglas) · 1926, 1929, 1929. Every line is from a document or a listed source. Nothing is filled in.

Janet Page Douglas, later Janet Crombie. Three crossings are on record. The ship for the first one is not known.

The Montroyal

Earlier nameEmpress of Britain (1906–1923); renamed Montroyal in 1924 [1]
BuilderFairfield Shipbuilding & Engineering Co., Govan; yard no. 442 [1][4]
Launched / maiden voyage11 November 1905; maiden voyage Liverpool–Quebec, 5 May 1906 [4]
Size14,189 gross as built; 15,646 gross after the 1924 refit; 548.8 ft × 65.7 ft [2]
Passengers after 1924600 cabin class and 800 third class [2]
EndHer final voyage began at Antwerp on 7 September 1929 — the same day the Duchess of Bedford left Greenock with Janet and Laurence — after 190 round trips [1]. Laid up September 1929 [2]; sold 17 June 1930 to the Stavanger Shipbreaking Co., Norway [1]. Her lounge was bought for the Sola Strand Hotel and became the Montroyal Ballroom [4].
