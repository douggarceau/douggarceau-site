A new book by Doug Garceau

The Names He Carried

The Laurence Brown Douglas Story

Born Lawrence Brown in Edinburgh in 1907, raised under another family's name, he crossed the Atlantic in 1929 and spent his life dressing shop windows from Chicago to Pawtucket. His grandson traced him back through a century of records.
