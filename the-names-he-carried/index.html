About the Author

Doug Garceau is Laurence Brown Douglas’s grandson. He researched and wrote The Names He Carried from census pages, ship records, civil registers and family documents, and built this site and its Academic Edition. He is a former rock music journalist from Seattle, where he wrote the column Raising Hell with Guitar Doug. He is a formally trained drummer and percussionist, with formal Berklee training in Ableton and recent Berklee drum set training with Rod Morgenstein of the Dixie Dregs and Winger. He is a working musician, with recent shows with So Many Rhodes, a Grateful Dead cover band. He founded Island News Media Productions LLC, which publishes this book.
