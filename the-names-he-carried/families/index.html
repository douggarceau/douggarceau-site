Are you family?

If your family tree includes any of these names, this story may hold records of your own people: their births, marriages, homes, work and photographs, gathered while tracing the life of Laurence Brown Douglas (1907–1961), born in Edinburgh and raised in Dunfermline, who died in Providence, Rhode Island.

We would love to hear from you. If you are descended from any of these families, or have photographs, letters or memories of them, please write to Doug Garceau, Laurence's grandson:

doug@islandnewsmedia.com
