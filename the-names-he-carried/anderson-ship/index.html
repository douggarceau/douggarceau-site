Mrs. Anderson's Ship, 1934

Elizabeth Anderson · Letitia · Quebec, September 30, 1934 · Port Huron, October 2, 1934. Every line is from a document or a listed source. Nothing is filled in.

Elizabeth Anderson, earlier Elizabeth Miller McDonald and Elizabeth Douglas, the mother named on Laurence's 1929 papers.

2 Oct 1934Port Huron, Mich. Anderson, Elizabeth, 60, F, housekeeper, born Edinburgh, Scotland; Scottish; reads yes; English. Money shown $60.00. Last residence Dunfermline, Scotland. Destination Chicago, Ill. Passage paid by self. Never before in the U.S. Joining "Daughter. Mrs Janet Crombie, 1518 Kostner Ave Chicago." Purpose "Per." Head tax status AD 6652. 5 ft 5 in; fair; fair hair; blue eyes. Nearest relative at home: "Cousin, Mrs Jenny Doig [?], Rose Cottage, Strathton [?]" (cut off). Admitted.front

2. Where the records disagree

  • Birthplace: "Edinburgh, Scotland" on this card; "Midlothian Gilmerton" on the 1911 and 1921 censuses (1921 census).
  • Chicago address: 1518 Kostner Ave on her 1934 card; 1534 So. Kostner Ave on the 1929 papers.
