1911 census: 16 Lochrin Place, Edinburgh

Census of Scotland, 1911, St Giles, Edinburgh, enumeration district 69, page 11 (685/4 69/11). George Square ward, St Cuthbert's parish. National Records of Scotland.

The entry is boxed in red and the names are highlighted. See the full page.

What it says

  • Schedule 1, 16 Lochrin Place, census night April 2, 1911. No head of household is listed.
  • Elizabeth Douglas, wife, 36, married 11 years, 2 children born alive, 2 living, born Gilmerton, Midlothian.
  • Janet, daughter, 10, at school, born Edinburgh.
  • Laurence B., son, 3, born Edinburgh.
  • Donald Steven (surname hard to read), boarder, 25, tailor, born Caithness.

Why it matters

This is the first record that calls him Laurence B. Douglas. Four years after his birth as Lawrence Brown, he is three years old and listed as a son in Elizabeth Douglas's home, half a mile from the hospital where he was born.
