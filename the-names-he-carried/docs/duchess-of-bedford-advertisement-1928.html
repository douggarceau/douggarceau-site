Canadian Pacific advertisement: Duchess Cruises to the West Indies, 1928

The Mitre (Bishop's University), November 1928, volume XXXVI, number 2, page 14. Advertisement by Canadian Pacific. Magazine scan: Bishop's University, ubishops.ca.

The complete printed page 14. The prices are for winter cruises from New York to the West Indies, not for the September 1929 Atlantic crossing.

What this document is

A full-page Canadian Pacific magazine advertisement from November 1928, offering winter cruises from New York on the new Duchess of Bedford.

What it says

  • "Sail for southern seas this winter on the Duchess of Bedford! This regal new ship has a 20,000 gross registered tonnage, high-pressure ventilation, real beds, separate sport decks and a whole deck of public rooms."
  • "Rates are as low as $200. for the Christmas holiday voyage and $300. for the longer cruises."
  • "THREE CRUISES — Dec. 22 (16 days). Jan. 10 (29 days). Feb. 11 (29 days). From New York."
  • Also on the page: Empress of Australia round the world (136 days, from $1,900); Duchess of Atholl to South America and Africa (104 days, from $1,500); Empress of Scotland to the Mediterranean (72 days, from $900).
