1911 census: Shieldhill Mains and Gean Tree Cottage, Libberton

Census of Scotland, 1911, Parish of Libberton, Lanarkshire, enumeration district 3, page 2 (650/3 ED 3 p. 2). National Records of Scotland.

The entry is boxed in red and the names are highlighted. See the full page.

What this document is

A census of Scotland. On census night a form was left at every home, and an enumerator copied each household into his book: names, ages, jobs and birthplaces.

What it says

  • Schedule 10, 3 Shieldhill Mains, three rooms: James Parker, head, 38, gamekeeper, born Biggar; Janet, wife, 34, married 7 years, 3 children; John, 5; Euphemia, 3; Robert, 1.
  • Schedule 12, Gean Tree Cottage: Andrew Rodger, 65, farmer, and his wife Jane, 46.

Why it matters

It shows James Parker still at Shieldhill in 1911, with his wife and children, and Gean Tree Cottage, the house Agnes named as her home in 1907, on the same page.
