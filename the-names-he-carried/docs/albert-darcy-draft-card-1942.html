1942 draft card: Albert Blake Darcy, Constance’s father

World War II draft registration, 1942, Rhode Island, serial U-40. U.S., World War II Draft Registration Cards, 1942. National Archives. Image via Ancestry.

The entry is marked in red and highlighted.

What this document is

A World War II draft registration card. In April 1942 every man aged 45 to 64 had to register; he filled in the card and signed it.

What it says

  • Albert Blake Darcy, 60 Blake Street, Shawomet, Kent County, R.I.; age 53.
  • Born November 20, 1888, at Rumford, R.I.
  • Employer: the Providence Journal, Providence.
  • Signed Albert B. Darcy.

Why it matters

It records where Constance’s father lived and worked during the war.
