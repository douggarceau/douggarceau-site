1929 arrival record: Lawrence Brown reaches Canada

Manifest of the S.S. Duchess of Bedford, continuation, list D, arriving September 13, 1929. U.S. National Archives, Record Group 85, M1464, roll 558, image 167.

The entry is marked in red and highlighted.

What it says

  • Line 10: Lawrence Brown.
  • Nearest relative in Scotland: his mother, Mrs. Elizabeth Anderson, 39 Grieve Street, Dunfermline.
  • Going to: his brother-in-law, Mr. Thomas Crombie, 1534 South Kostner Avenue, Chicago.

Why it matters

It names the family he left behind in Dunfermline and the family waiting for him in Chicago.
