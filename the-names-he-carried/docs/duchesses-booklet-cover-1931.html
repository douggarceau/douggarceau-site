Canadian Pacific booklet cover: "The Duchesses"

Cover reproduced by GG Archives with its Duchess of Bedford cabin passenger list of 4 September 1931, Liverpool to Quebec and Montreal via Belfast and Greenock, Captain H. Sibbons. ggarchives.com.

Colour cover reading "Canadian Pacific" and "THE DUCHESSES," with a two-funnelled liner at sea. From a 1931 sailing, two years after Laurence and Janet's crossing.

What this document is

The illustrated cover Canadian Pacific used for its Duchess liners. This copy is from 1931; it is not from Laurence and Janet's September 1929 sailing.

What it says

  • "Canadian Pacific"
  • "THE DUCHESSES"
