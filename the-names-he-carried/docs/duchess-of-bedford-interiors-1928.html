Dining saloon on A Deck, looking aft, 1928. NRS UCS1/116/31/16.

Drawing and Observation Room on the Promenade Deck, looking toward the port side, 1928. NRS UCS1/116/31/3.

Cabin No. 103, three berths, A Deck, 1928. NRS UCS1/116/31/22. Not known to be Laurence's or Janet's cabin.

What this document is

Builder's photographs of the new ship, taken in 1928, the year she entered service. They are from the records of the shipbuilder, held by the National Records of Scotland.

What it says

  • The dining saloon on A Deck.
  • The Drawing and Observation Room on the Promenade Deck.
  • Cabin 103 on A Deck, with three berths, a washbasin and a porthole.
