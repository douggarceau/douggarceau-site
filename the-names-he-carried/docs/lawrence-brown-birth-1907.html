Birth of Lawrence Brown, Edinburgh, 1907

Statutory register of births, District of St Giles, City of Edinburgh, 1907, page 280, entry 839 (685/04 0839). National Records of Scotland.

The entry is boxed in red and the names are highlighted. See the full page.

What this document is

A Scottish birth register entry. Every birth in Scotland had to be registered within a few weeks; the registrar wrote the child's name, date and place of birth, the parents, and the informant, who signed.

What it says

  • Name: Lawrence Brown, male. Marked illegitimate.
  • Born: June 14, 1907, 9 p.m., Royal Maternity Hospital, Edinburgh.
  • Father: the column is left blank.
  • Mother: Agnes Brown, domestic servant. Domicile: Gean Cottage, Shieldhill, Biggar.
  • Informant: Agnes Brown, Mother. Registered at Edinburgh, July 4, 1907, by R. D. Robertson, registrar.

Why it matters

This is Laurence's own birth, under the name he was born with. It names his mother, Agnes Brown, and her home, Gean Cottage at Shieldhill, the row at Quothquan where she grew up. The father's column is empty: the question the whole book follows starts on this page.
