1891 census: the "Castle" row, Quothquan, Libberton

Census night, April 5, 1891. Parish of Libberton, Lanarkshire, enumeration district 3, pages 1–2. Address given for every household: Castle.

What this document is

A census of Scotland. On census night a form was left at every home, and an enumerator copied each household into his book: names, ages, jobs and birthplaces.
