Marriage of James Lawson Parker and Janet Crawford Stewart, Carluke, 1903

Statutory register of marriages, Parish of Carluke, County of Lanark, 1903, page 27, entry 54 (629/54). National Records of Scotland.

The entry is boxed in red and the names are highlighted. See the full page.

What this document is

A Scottish marriage register entry, written by the registrar after the wedding, giving both partners' ages, jobs, addresses and parents, the minister, and the witnesses.

What it says

  • Married: August 28, 1903, at Coldstream, Carluke, after banns according to the forms of the United Free Church.
  • Groom: James Lawson Parker, 31, gamekeeper, bachelor, of Shieldhill, Biggar. Parents Benjamin Parker, mason (deceased), and Jane Lawson, lady's maid (deceased).
  • Bride: Janet Crawford Stewart, 24, domestic servant, spinster, of Coldstream. Parents John Stewart, shepherd, and Euphemia Stewart, maiden surname Wallace.
  • Minister: John White, St John's Church, Carluke. Witnesses George Affleck and Mary Stewart. Registered August 31, 1903.

Why it matters

James Parker was raised as an "adopted" son in the cottage where Agnes grew up and lived under the same roof as her in 1891 and 1901. This shows he was gamekeeper at Shieldhill and a married man by 1903, years before Laurence was born.
