Canadian Pacific Atlantic track chart

From the same GG Archives Duchess of Bedford material of 4 September 1931. ggarchives.com.

The complete chart as published. Two brown marks near the top and middle of the map are on the scanned paper. The small print is limited by the scan.

What this document is

A printed chart given to passengers, showing Canadian Pacific's Atlantic routes between Britain and Canada, a table of approximate distances, and blank lines to log the ship's daily position. It is from 1931, not from the 1929 voyage.

What it says

  • "CANADIAN PACIFIC ATLANTIC TRACK CHART."
  • Log table headed "Date · Distance · Lat. · Long. · Remarks" (blank).
  • "APPROXIMATE DISTANCES." in nautical miles, including routes from Liverpool, Glasgow, Southampton, Belfast and Greenock to Quebec, Saint John and Montreal. Individual figures are too small to read on this scan.
