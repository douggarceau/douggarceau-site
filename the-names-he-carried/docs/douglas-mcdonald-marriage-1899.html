Marriage of Robert Douglas and Elizabeth Millar McDonald, Edinburgh, 1899

Statutory register of marriages, District of St Giles, City of Edinburgh, 1899, page 107, entry 213 (685/4 213). National Records of Scotland.

The entry is boxed in red and the names are highlighted. See the full page.

What this document is

A Scottish marriage register entry, written by the registrar after the wedding, giving both partners' ages, jobs, addresses and parents, the minister, and the witnesses.

What it says

  • Married: June 9, 1899, at 34 Saint Patrick Square, Edinburgh, after publication according to the forms of the Free Church of Scotland.
  • Groom: Robert Douglas, 23, saw miller, bachelor, 29 Saint Leonard's Terrace. Parents Archibald Douglas, forester, and Isabella Douglas, maiden surname Gray.
  • Bride: Elizabeth Miller McDonald, 24, domestic servant, spinster, 4 Hatton Place. Named parents John McDonald, coal salesman, and Janet McDonald, maiden surname Page.
  • Minister: W. Whyte Smith, B.D., Newington Free Church. Witnesses James Finlayson and Janet Page Moodie. Registered June 12, 1899.

Why it matters

These are the two people who raised Laurence. Elizabeth is the woman he always named as his mother. Their own daughter, Janet Page Douglas, was born the next year and later crossed to America with him.
