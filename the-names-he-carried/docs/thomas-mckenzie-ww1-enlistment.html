First World War enlistment: Thomas McKenzie, Agnes Brown’s husband

British Army service record, descriptive report on enlistment, Thomas McKenzie. UK, British Army World War I Service Records, 1914–1920. The National Archives (UK). Image via Ancestry.

The entry is marked in red and highlighted.

What this document is

A British Army enlistment form. When a man joined up, an officer measured him and wrote down his next of kin, his marriage and his children.

What it says

  • Thomas McKenzie, apparent age 26, height six feet.
  • Next of kin: his wife, Agnes McKenzie, of Edinburgh; later care of Thomson, Sherwood, Bonnyrigg, Midlothian.
  • Married Agnes Brown, spinster, in Edinburgh, August 1911 (the form reads the 25th; the marriage register reads the 28th).
  • Children: Mary, born May 6, 1912, and Jean, born August 14, 1914, both in Edinburgh.

Why it matters

It shows Agnes Brown’s new family: her husband in the army and her two daughters, Laurence’s half-sisters.
