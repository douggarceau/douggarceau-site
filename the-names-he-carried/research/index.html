Proof Summaries

1. Lawrence Brown (born 1907) and Laurence Brown Douglas (died 1961) are the same man

Status: proved

  • Direct evidence: 1943 naturalization petition states lawful entry at Detroit on 15 Sep 1929 "under the name of Lawrence Brown."
  • Birth date 14 June 1907 is consistent on the 1907 birth register, 1912 school register, 1939 marriage license and 1940 draft card.
  • The 1929 Lawrence Brown gives Dunfermline and the Douglas household (Elizabeth Anderson, Janet and Thomas Crombie) as his family.
  • No conflicting record found.

2. Identity of Laurence's father

Status: unresolved

  • Direct evidence: none. Father's column on the birth register is blank.
  • Indirect evidence: mother's domicile on the birth entry was Gean Cottage, Shieldhill, Biggar, on the estate of Nathaniel Dunlop (knighted July 1907).
  • Persons of interest in the files: Sir Nathaniel Dunlop; his nephew Dr Binnie Dunlop; Alexander Archibald, the Shieldhill gardener. No record links any of them to Agnes Brown.
  • Test available: autosomal DNA comparison between Laurence's descendants and descendants of Archibald Dunlop (1790–1865).
