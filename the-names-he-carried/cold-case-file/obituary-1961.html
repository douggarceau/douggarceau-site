LAURENCE B. DOUGLAS
Laurence B. Douglas, 53, who worked for firms in Pawtucket during the past 15 years, died Thursday in St. Joseph's Hospital, Providence, after a brief illness. He resided at 644 River avenue, Providence.
He was born in Edinburgh, Scotland, a son of the late Robert and Elizabeth (Anderson) Douglas. He had been employed by Bond Clothing Co. and later was in business for himself as a window display man.
Besides his widow, Constance (Darcy) Douglas, he leaves three daughters, Sister Mary Laurene, O.P., of St. Dominic's Convent, Blauvelt, N. Y., Mrs. Constance Garceau of Warwick and Miss Norma Douglas of Providence, and a sister, Mrs. Thomas Crombie of Chicago.
A funeral service will be held at 10 a. m., Monday from the Ruth E. Urquhart mortuary, 812 Greenwich avenue, Warwick.
