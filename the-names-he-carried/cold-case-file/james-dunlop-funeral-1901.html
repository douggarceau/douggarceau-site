Item 102 · March 18, 1901

Funeral of Dr James Dunlop

Source: Newspaper report published March 18, 1901. Found in the British Newspaper Archive (research pass of November 25, 2026). The archive files this notice under an unrelated heading, “Cost of Queen’s Funeral”; the printed heading is “Funeral of Dr James Dunlop.”

The full checked transcription and the British Newspaper Archive page link for this article are in the research report “British_Newspaper_Archive_Research_2026-11-25.pdf” and will be added here word for word once that report is placed in the project. The details below are from the report’s findings.

What the record says

Describes the funeral of Dr. James Dunlop from his residence in Carlton Place to Sighthill Cemetery, Glasgow.

Names Nathaniel Dunlop and Matthew Dunlop among the mourners.

Says the funeral took place on Saturday afternoon, which places it on March 16, 1901.

Why it matters

Places Nathaniel in Glasgow at his brother’s funeral and adds the cemetery and procession to the record of James’s death.
