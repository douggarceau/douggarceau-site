How Do We Know All This?

Every historical claim should lead to an identifiable document. This register organizes the Cold Case File's source entries by the date of the event or record, and links to the underlying exhibit and available source materials. Index transcriptions, photographs of original records, and interpretive research are not interchangeable.

Evidence standard: A document can establish a birth, address, occupation, or reported public event without proving an inferred motive, relationship, or payment.
