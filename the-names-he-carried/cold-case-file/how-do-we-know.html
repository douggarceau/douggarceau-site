How Do We Know All This?

Every historical claim should lead to an identifiable document. This register organizes the Cold Case File's source entries by the date of the event or record, and links to the underlying exhibit and available source materials. Index transcriptions, photographs of original records, and interpretive research are not interchangeable.

Evidence standard: A document can establish a birth, address, occupation, or reported public event without proving an inferred motive, relationship, or payment. Unverified theories are identified separately in the case presentations. The source list is a living inventory, not a claim that every theory has been proved.

Current register is loaded from the Cold Case File's master evidence inventory (items.json). New items added to that inventory appear here automatically. Dates are historical event dates where recorded; newspaper publication dates should be distinguished within the exhibits.
