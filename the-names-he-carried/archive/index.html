Scope and content

The collection documents the birth of Lawrence Brown at the Royal Maternity Hospital, Edinburgh, on 14 June 1907; his mother Agnes Brown and her family in Peeblesshire and Lanarkshire; the Douglas household that raised him in Edinburgh and Dunfermline; the Shieldhill estate and the Dunlop family; his emigration to the United States in 1929; and his life in Chicago, San Francisco, Pittsburgh and Rhode Island until his death in 1961.

Arrangement

Documents are arranged in nine series by record type, and within each series by date. Each document has a permanent catalog number (DOC-001 to DOC-100) matching its Cold Case File item number.

  • Series 1. Civil registration (births, marriages, deaths) (19 documents)
  • Series 2. Census and population registers (34 documents)
  • Series 3. Immigration, travel and border crossing (13 documents)
  • Series 4. Military and naturalization (5 documents)
  • Series 5. Directories and electoral rolls (13 documents)
  • Series 6. Probate, wills and reference works (9 documents)
  • Series 7. Newspapers and obituaries (4 documents)
  • Series 8. School records (1 documents)
  • Series 9. Weather records (2 documents)

Open to all readers. Cite this collection as: Doug Garceau, comp., The Names He Carried: Academic Edition (https://douggarceau.com/the-names-he-carried/archive/).

Standards

Research follows the Genealogical Proof Standard. Citations follow the model of Elizabeth Shown Mills, Evidence Explained. Sources are classified as original, derivative or authored; information as primary or secondary; evidence as direct, indirect or negative.
