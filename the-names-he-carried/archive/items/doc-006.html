1886. DEATHS in the District of Peebles in the County of Peebles
[Column headings, printed] No. | Name and Surname. Rank or Profession, and whether Single, Married, or Widowed. | When and Where Died. | Sex. | Age. | Name, Surname, & Rank or Profession of Father. Name, and Maiden Surname of Mother. | Cause of Death, Duration of Disease, and Medical Attendant by whom certified. | Signature & Qualification of Informant, and Residence, if out of the House in which the Death occurred. | When and where Registered, and Signature of Registrar.

No. 13
Name: Margaret Jane Foster (Single)
Died: 1886. March Thirty First 4h. 50m. P.M. 59 Northgate Peebles
Sex: F. Age: 10 Months
Father/Mother: Arthur Foster Mason Journeyman. Margaret Foster M.S. Conlay
Cause: Bronchitis 21 days Dentition as Cert by Alexander Ferguson M.D.
Informant: Arthur Foster Father
Registered: 1886. April 1st At Peebles Geo Morison Registrar.

No. 14
Name: George Brown Ploughman (Married to Christina Henderson)
Died: 1886. April Fifth 1h. 30m. AM. Edston Peebles
Sex: M. Age: 23 Years
Father/Mother: William Brown Ploughman. Agnes Brown M.S. McGarth
Cause: Pleuro Pneumonia 21 days Asthenia as Cert by Alexander Ferguson M.D.
Informant: William Brown Father. Harrow – Stobo – [a word below, illegible]
Registered: 1886. April 5th At Peebles Geo Morison Registrar.

No. 15
Name: William Mathison General Labourer (Widower of Agnes Grossart)
Died: 1886. April Eleventh 1h. 0m. A.M. Old Town Peebles
Sex: M. Age: 81 Years
Father/Mother: Wm Mathison Cotton Weaver (deceased). Helen Mathison M.S. Brown (deceased)
Cause: Senile debility as Cert by Clement B Gunn M.D.
Informant: John Mathison Son (Present) Old Town Peebles
Registered: 1886. April 12th At Peebles Geo Morison Registrar.

[Signature] Geo Morison Registrar.
