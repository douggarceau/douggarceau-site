No. 615
When/where/how: 1911. on the Twenty sixth day of August at 34 Chambers Street Edinburgh. By Declaration in presence of Alexander Bruce, pattern weaver, and Jane Bruce his wife
Parties: William W Blackhall, Hotel Boots (Bachelor), 25, Royal Hotel Edinburgh. Margaret B Bruce, Tweed Winder (Spinster), 23, 2 Carnarvon Street Hawick
Fathers/Mothers: William Blackhall Foreman Carter. Mary Blackhall M.S. Wood. Alexander Bruce Tweed Pattern Weaver. Jane Bruce M.S. Laing
Warrant of Sheriff-Substitute of the Lothians and Peebles, dated 26 August 1911.
Registered: 1911. August 26 At Edinburgh R D Robertson Registrar.

No. 616
When/where/how: 1911. on the Twenty eighth day of August at 34 Chambers Street Edinburgh. By Declaration in presence of Edward Thomson, Asylum Attendant, and John McIntyre, Coachman
Parties: Thomas McKay McKenzie, Asylum Attendant (Bachelor), 21, West House Royal Asylum Morningside Edinburgh. Agnes McGarth Brown, Asylum Nurse (Spinster), 25, Same place
Fathers/Mothers: John Alexander McKenzie Coastguardsman. Mary McKenzie M.S. McKay (deceased). George Brown Farm Greive (deceased). Christina Brown M.S. Henderson (deceased)
Warrant of Sheriff-Substitute of the Lothians and Peebles, dated 28 August 1911.
Registered: 1911. August 28 At Edinburgh R D Robertson Registrar.

[Signature] R D Robertson Registrar.
