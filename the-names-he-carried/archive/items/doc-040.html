Transcription, word for word

Everything as written, including misspellings. [?] marks an uncertain reading. Struck-through words are marked as struck through. Notes in square brackets describe the page and are not part of it.

[Printed chart. Map of the North Atlantic from eastern Canada to Britain, France, Spain and North Africa, with route lines; latitude and longitude figures around the border. Place names on the map include CANADA, ENGLAND, FRANCE, BAY OF BISCAY, SPAIN, AFRICA, MEDITERRANEAN SEA; the smaller names are too small to read on this scan. Two brown marks on the paper, one at the top centre of the map and one at the middle.]

CANADIAN PACIFIC
ATLANTIC TRACK CHART.

[Log table, blank] Date | Distance | Lat. | Long. | Remarks

APPROXIMATE DISTANCES. Nautical Miles
[Three columns of routes and mileages, including from Liverpool to Quebec and Saint John, N.B.; Southampton to Quebec and Saint John; Glasgow to Quebec; Quebec to Montreal; Liverpool to Belfast; Glasgow to Greenock; Antwerp to Southampton; Cherbourg to Southampton. The figures are too small to read reliably on this scan and are not transcribed.]
