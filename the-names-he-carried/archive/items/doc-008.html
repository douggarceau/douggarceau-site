—Page 1.—
[Top left, handwritten] VA
1895. DEATHS in the Parish of Stobo in the County of Peebles.
[Column headings, printed, as on all Scottish death registers]

No. 1
Name: William Brown Ploughman (Married to Agnes McGarth)
Died: 1895. January Thirteenth 7h. 30m. P.M. The Harrow in the Parish of Stobo.
Sex: M. Age: 59 yrs.
Father/Mother: William Brown Ploughman (deceased). Agnes Brown M.S. McWhay[?] (deceased)
Cause: Heart Disease. Pneumonia. As certified by Clement B. Gunn M.D.
Informant: William Brown Son (Present)
Registered: 1895. January 14th At Stobo Andw Jervis Registrar.

No. 2
Name: James Thomson Farm Grieve (Widower of Mary Samuel)
Died: 1895. February First 1h. 40m. P.M. Stobo Mill in the Parish of Stobo.
Sex: M. Age: 73 yrs.
Father/Mother: Adam Thomson Agricultural Labourer (deceased). Agnes Thomson M.S. Young (deceased)
Cause: Apoplexy – 10 days. As certified by John Connel M.D.
Informant: John Thomson Son (Present)
Registered: 1895. February 6th At Stobo Andw Jervis Registrar.

No. 3
Name: James Currie Pauper formerly a Shoemaker (Widower of Annie Robertson)
Died: 1895. March Twentyfifth 10h. 15m. A.M. Dreva Cottage in the Parish of Stobo.
Sex: M. Age: 73 yrs.
Father/Mother: [lines drawn, no entry]
Cause: General debility. Old age. Not Certified
Informant: William Laghtane[?] Son-in-Law (present)
Registered: 1895. March 27th At Stobo Andw Jervis Registrar.

[Signature, cut off at the bottom] Andw Jervis Registrar.
