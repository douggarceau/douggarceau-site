Transcription — Binnie Dunlop and Charles Robertson Dunlop entries

The two entries are transcribed below. The complete printed page is shown above. Text in square brackets describes the page.

[Printed page 248; heading: SEPTEMBER 1888]

Dunlop, Binnie, born Aug. 3rd, 1874. Left Midsummer, 1891.
Son of J. Dunlop, M.D., Glasgow. Dickson's.

Glasgow Univ., M.B., Ch.B., 1898. House Surgeon, Victoria Infirmary,
Glasgow. Served in S. Africa, 1901-02. Charteris House, Woodford Green,
Essex.

Dunlop, Charles Robertson, born Jan. 9th, 1876. Left Christmas,
1891. Son of J. Dunlop, M.D., Glasgow. Dickson's.

Glasgow Univ. M.A. 1893. Balliol Coll., Oxford, 1894. 2nd Class Finals
Jurisprudence. B.A. 1897. B.C.L. 1900 (1st Class). Barrister-at-Law,
1901. 2 Paper Buildings, Temple, London, E.C.
