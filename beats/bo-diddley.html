The Bo Diddley Beat

The Bo Diddley beat is the clave rhythm (think "shave and a haircut") played as a rock and roll groove. It drives Bo Diddley's own records and songs like "Not Fade Away," and on the kit the floor tom usually carries it.

How it counts

Floor tom on 1, the a of 1, the & of 2, the & of 3, and 4. Hi-hat on every eighth note. Count 1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a.

Learn it in three steps

  1. Clap the rhythm while counting sixteenths out loud. It is five hits: 1, a, &, &, 4.
  2. Play it on the floor tom alone.
  3. Add the hi-hat eighth notes in the other hand.

Watch out for

  • Turning the a of 1 into a straight eighth note. It is the last sixteenth of the beat.
  • Losing count in beat 3, where the rhythm starts on the &.
