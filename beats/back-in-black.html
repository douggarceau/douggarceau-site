FAMOUS BEATS · BEGINNER

AC/DC: The "Back in Black" Beat

Phil Rudd's playing on "Back in Black" is the model for rock drumming that serves the song. It is a straight-eighth rock beat with nothing added, and it hits hard because it sits so steadily at around 94 BPM.

Hear it on: AC/DC, Back in Black (1980)

How it counts

Hi-hat eighth notes, bass drum on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4. Count 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &.

Learn it in three steps

  1. Play the beat at 80 until the snare lands the same every time.
  2. Bring it up to about 94, the tempo of the record.
  3. Try accenting the hi-hat on the numbers (1, 2, 3, 4) and keeping the & notes softer. That is where the rock swagger comes from.

Watch out for

  • Playing the snare lighter than the bass drum. In rock, the backbeat is the loudest thing on the kit.
  • Speeding up in the chorus. Great rock drummers stay put.
