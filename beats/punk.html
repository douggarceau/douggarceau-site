The Punk Skank Beat

The skank beat is the engine of fast punk: bass drum on every beat and snare on every &, so the two alternate like a polka. It sounds simple, but holding it together at 180 and above takes real stamina.

How it counts

Bass drum on 1, 2, 3, 4. Snare on every &. Hi-hat or ride on the beats. Count 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &.

Learn it in three steps

  1. Start at 120: bass drum on the numbers, snare on the &s.
  2. Add the hi-hat with the bass drum on the numbers.
  3. Add 10 BPM at a time. When it gets messy, drop back 20 and build again.

Watch out for

  • Tight shoulders. Speed comes from staying loose.
  • The snare drifting early. Keep it exactly halfway between the beats.
