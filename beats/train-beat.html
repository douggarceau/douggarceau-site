FAMOUS BEATS · INTERMEDIATE

The Train Beat

The train beat keeps the snare moving in sixteenth notes like wheels on a track, with accents on 2 and 4. You can hear its chug on Johnny Cash records like "Folsom Prison Blues," and it is often played with brushes.

Hear it on: Johnny Cash, At Folsom Prison (1968)

How it counts

Alternating sixteenths on the snare (right, left, right, left). Accent 2 and 4, keep the rest soft. Bass drum on 1 and 3.

Learn it in three steps

  1. Sixteenths on the snare, completely even and soft, at 70.
  2. Accent beats 2 and 4. Only those two notes get louder.
  3. Add the bass drum on 1 and 3, then bring the tempo up.

Watch out for

  • All notes getting loud. The contrast between soft and accented is the sound.
  • Tensing up at faster tempos. Keep the sticks low.
