AMADEUS SOUND STUDIO

Beat Maker

Pick a kit, tap the squares to build a pattern, and press Play. Each row is one sound and each column is one sixteenth note. Four columns make one beat.

START FROM

Swipe the grid sideways to see all 16 steps.

Tap a row's name to hear it. M mutes a row. Turn on Rolls & notes, then tap a lit square: on drum rows it adds a fast roll (2, 3 or 4 hits, the trap hi-hat sound), and on bass rows it changes the note. Space bar plays and stops.

What the kits are

Hip-hop & Trap has a punchy kick, snare, clap, rim, hi-hats and a long 808 bass that you can play in any key. Electronic has house and techno drums, a crash, a percussion hit, a synth bass and an acid line. Acoustic drums is a real drum set recorded with the same sounds as the interactive kit on this site, including the ride cymbal and the hi-hat played with the foot.
