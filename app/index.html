THE PHONE APP

AMADEUS ON YOUR PHONE

One tap from your home screen. Opens full screen, no browser bars, and pages you've opened keep working without a signal.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open douggarceau.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom (top right on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Amadeus is now on your home screen.

Android

  1. Open douggarceau.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the app above, or the ⋮ menu and Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. It shows up with your other apps.
Full screenFeels like a real app: its own icon, no address bar.
Works offlineLessons, grooves and drum sounds you've opened stay on your phone.
Always currentNews, videos and On Tour update when you're online.

On a computer? Chrome and Edge can install it too: look for the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
