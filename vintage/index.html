VINTAGE DRUMS · THE SHOP GUIDE

10 of America’s best vintage drum shops

Where to find old Ludwigs, Slingerland Radio Kings, vintage Zildjians and the people who know them best. Our picks are based on vintage inventory, specialist knowledge, restoration work and standing in the drum community. This is an editorial selection, not an official ranking.

01

Steve Maxwell Vintage & Custom Drums

Glendale Heights, Illinois

maxwelldrums.com →

The story

Steve Maxwell dates his first shop to 2001: a roughly 700-square-foot room in Chicago's Fine Arts Building, open on Sundays while he kept his technology career. The business grew inside that building, moved its Illinois operation to Naperville, and in April 2016 relocated to a 7,500-square-foot space in Glendale Heights. Maxwell also opened a Manhattan store, which became Good Hands (No. 7). Some directories say 2002; Maxwell's own account says 2001.

The owner

Steve is a drummer, collector, appraiser and broker who began collecting rare drums in the mid-1970s. His business grew around historically important instruments, including gear owned by well-known musicians, and reproductions of hard-to-find vintage hardware.

Known for

High-end vintage drums and cymbals, artist-owned instruments, Gretsch, Craviotto and vintage reproduction parts. A favorite of collectors and of players chasing classic jazz sounds.

02

Revival Drum Shop

Portland, Oregon

revivaldrumshop.com →

The story

José Medeles and fellow vintage-drum enthusiast Scott McPherson opened Revival in 2009 to give drummers a welcoming place to discover unusual instruments and meet each other. Medeles had moved to Portland in 2008 after years of touring. Lessons, clinics and performances became part of the shop's role in the community.

The owner

José Medeles is a working drummer and author whose credits include the Breeders, Ben Harper and 1939 Ensemble. He previously worked at Professional Drum Shop in Hollywood. His books include The Stoic Drummer, and his approach puts musical exploration and community first.

Known for

Curated vintage kits and snares, distinctive cymbals, specialty percussion and modern instruments with real character. A go-to for unusual recording and performance sounds, not just collecting.

Hawthorne Drum Shop

McKees Rocks, Pennsylvania

hawthornedrumshop.com →

The story

Founded in winter 2016, Hawthorne earned a Modern Drummer retailer profile by February 2020 for its fast rise as a respected vintage dealer. It now operates on the second floor of Hollowood Music near Pittsburgh, combining retail with repairs, rentals and lessons.

The owner

Owner Chris Hawthorne is the shop's main public voice. He has appeared in educational videos on identifying older instruments, buying vintage gear, and which modifications affect sound and value.

Known for

A wide range of vintage kits, snares, single drums, cymbals and parts, including Ludwig and Slingerland drums, older Yamaha kits and vintage Avedis Zildjian cymbals. Gear for collectors and working players alike.

Address601 Chartiers Avenue, Floor 2, McKees Rocks, PA 15136

04

Wood & Weather Drum Shop

South Hadley, Massachusetts

woodandweatherdrumshop.com →

The story

Joseph Barthelette founded Wood & Weather in summer 2015, working from rented space in an Indian Orchard industrial building and growing through online sales and drum shows. He began exhibiting at the Chicago Drum Show in 2016. The South Hadley storefront held its grand opening on June 25, 2021, with more room for inventory, restoration and lessons.

The owner

Barthelette started drumming at fourteen. An early find, a blue-sparkle 1960s Ludwig kit that he cleaned up and resold, helped turn the hobby into a business built on travel, dealer relationships and collection purchases.

Known for

Vintage American kits and snares, unusual finishes, single drums, older Japanese instruments and a broad cymbal selection, plus modern gear and restoration and repair work.

Nelson Drum Shop

Nashville, Tennessee

nelsondrumshop.com →

The story

Nelson grew out of Bryson Nelson buying and selling vintage drums from home after moving from Southern California to Tennessee. Friends came over to try instruments, and those visits grew into a retail business that began around 2015. In 2021 it expanded into a larger shop in East Nashville.

The owner

Bryson Nelson toured professionally for several years and was already trading vintage drums on the road. He has said his priorities for the shop are community, events and a place where musicians can meet and feel inspired.

Known for

Vintage and classic drum sets, used snares, cymbals and unusual percussion, alongside boutique modern makers like Sugar Percussion. Inventory has included older Ludwig and Tama gear.

06

Drugan's Drums & Guitars

Des Plaines, Illinois

drugansdrums.com →

The story

Drugan's was operating by 2014, when the Chicago Drum Show program listed the business at its current Cora Street address. The family business grew around buying, selling and trading vintage and modern instruments. In 2025, Johnny and Brian Drugan took over the Chicago Drum Show from Rob Cook.

The owner

Brothers Johnny and Brian Drugan run the family operation. Between the shop and the Chicago Drum Show, they sit at the center of the vintage collecting world.

Known for

Classic American drums, especially Ludwig and Slingerland, plus vintage cymbals and the sounds of jazz, blues and early rock. Recent inventory has included 1960s Ludwig kits and 1940s–50s Slingerland Radio Kings, alongside modern pro gear.

Address1577 S. Cora Street, Des Plaines, IL 60018

Good Hands Drum Shop

New York, New York

goodhandsdrumshop.com →

The story

Good Hands is the successor to Steve Maxwell's New York City shop. Jess Birch, the founding manager of Maxwell's NYC store, opened Good Hands in 2022. Its new website launched in September 2023.

The owner

Jess Birch got hooked on drums at ten and has spent more than thirty-five years with percussion on stage and off. His approach is practical knowledge, fair dealing, and matching the instrument to the player's needs and budget.

Known for

Curated vintage instruments, used pro gear, consignments and distinctive cymbals. In June 2024 the shop turned its main cymbal-testing room over to custom and handmade cymbals.

08

A Drummer's Tradition

San Rafael, California

adrummerstradition.com →

The story

Robert Bowler opened A Drummer's Tradition in 1996, according to the shop and a 2014 San Francisco Chronicle profile. He found instruments through personal contacts, schools and garage sales, building a business in vintage, custom and new percussion, with repair, restoration and lessons.

The owner

Bowler is a drummer, collector and restoration specialist. He started playing in high school, got into drum history in his twenties, and is known for hands-on repair work and tracking down obscure replacement parts.

Known for

Vintage American drums, collectible snares, custom instruments and restoration, including 1920s Ludwig and Slingerland snares and 1960s Ludwig kits. Great for players who want to play old drums and keep them alive.

09

Memphis Drum Shop

Memphis, Tennessee

memphisdrumshop.com →

The story

Jim and Nancy Pettit opened Memphis Drum Shop in 1987 on Madison Avenue and moved to the Cooper-Young district in 1999. The shop grew online and became known for its demonstration videos, including the MyCymbal approach of letting you hear the exact cymbal you're buying. Today it has a Vintage Vault, Snare Drum Vault, Cymbal Vault and Gong Chamber.

The owner

Jim Pettit started drumming at twelve and toured before going into retail; his growing collection helped spark the shop. Nancy, a former schoolteacher, has worked alongside him from the start and leads sound programs using gongs and other instruments.

Known for

A dedicated vintage collection inside a full pro drum store, an exceptionally deep cymbal selection, snares and gongs, and detailed sound demos that help you choose.

Address878 S. Cooper Street, Memphis, TN 38104
HoursMonday–Saturday 10 a.m.–5 p.m. · Sunday closed · Gong Chamber by appointment

Fork's Drum Closet

Nashville, Tennessee

forksdrumcloset.com →

The story

Gary Forkum founded Fork's in 1982 with a small collection of drums inside Corner Music in Berry Hill. In 1991 it moved next door on 12th Avenue South, where it stayed for twenty-seven years. After Forkum retired in 2018, Steve Maxwell bought the business and moved it to 308 Chestnut Street. Longtime manager Daniel Douchette bought the store at the start of 2026, returning it to local ownership.

The owner

Daniel Douchette is a longtime Fork's employee and manager who became the owner after years inside the business.

Known for

A full-line drum store with a broad pro selection, used gear, vintage replacement parts and repairs. The pick for players who want vintage support alongside modern working gear.
