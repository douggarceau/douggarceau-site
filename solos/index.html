LEVEL 3 · THE MASTERS

THE DRUM SOLO

A great solo is not a collection of fast licks. It is music: it follows the melody, tells a story and never loses the form. Learn it from the masters.

01 · KNOW THE MELODYSing the tune in your head while you play.
02 · PHRASE LIKE A HORNLet the tune’s phrases shape yours.
03 · KEEP THE FORMThe band must always know where you are.
04 · BUILDStart simple, leave space, and climb to a climax.
Elvin Jones

Building a solo from the melody

Elvin explains and demonstrates the idea at the heart of a great solo: know the tune, and let its melody and phrases shape what you play. A solo is a song, not a string of licks.

Papa Jo Jones

“Caravan,” 1957

The man who moved time from the bass drum to the hi-hat and ride. Watch the touch, the space, the humor, and how much music he gets from a small kit.

Papa Jo Jones

“Caravan,” 1978

Two decades later, with close-ups of his hands and sticks. Same tune, still building the solo like a story.

Joe Morello

“Take Five” with Dave Brubeck

A solo in 5/4. Morello starts quietly and builds to a climax his hands are almost too fast to film, with flawless left-hand technique and the form never lost.

◉ Get the record: The Dave Brubeck Quartet, Time Out → Watch on YouTube →
Neil Peart · Rush

“YYZ,” Moving Pictures tour, 1981

Filmed in Montreal for Exit…Stage Left. The rock solo as composition: themes that return, orchestral percussion woven in, and every section planned and built like a piece of music.

◉ Get the record: Rush, Exit…Stage Left and Moving Pictures → Watch on YouTube →
John Bonham · Led Zeppelin

“Moby Dick,” Royal Albert Hall, 1970

The rock drum solo everyone pictures: huge sound, deep groove, and the famous section played with his bare hands. Bonham never loses the feel, even at his most explosive.

◉ Get the record: Led Zeppelin, Led Zeppelin II and the Led Zeppelin DVD → Watch on YouTube →
Peter Criss · KISS

“100,000 Years,” Detroit, 1975

From the Cobo Arena shows that became Alive!. Pure arena rock: big fills, showmanship and swing from a drummer raised on Gene Krupa and big-band jazz.
