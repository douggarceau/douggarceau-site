The 40 Drum Rudiments

Rudiments are the vocabulary of drumming: the standard sticking patterns every drummer practices. These are the 40 International Drum Rudiments from the Percussive Arts Society, in four families. Each page has notation with sticking, play-along audio with the right hand on the right speaker and the left hand on the left, and tips for practicing it.
